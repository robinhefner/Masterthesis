# [[Gonzales et al., 2023.pdf|Synthetic data in health care: A narrative review]]

### Research Gap:
> ([[Gonzales et al., 2023.pdf#page=2&annotation=926R|Gonzales et al., 2023, p.2]])
> The goal of this review article is to serve as a guide for researchers, data entrepreneurs, and innovators to improve understanding of the utility, value, and appropriateness of [[Synthetische Patiendendaten|Synthetic data ]] for their respective applications.

### Methodik:

> ([[Gonzales et al., 2023.pdf#page=3&annotation=934R|Gonzales et al., 2023, p.3]])
> We conducted a narrative review of existing literature using PubMed and Scopus.
> ([[Gonzales et al., 2023.pdf#page=3&annotation=937R|Gonzales et al., 2023, p.3]])
> An additional targeted search was conducted using Google Scholar and Google Search engine to gather additional information from grey literature, including those related to the examples that were highlighted in this paper.
> ([[Gonzales et al., 2023.pdf#page=3&annotation=940R|Gonzales et al., 2023, p.3]])
> After screening the 4,226 unique articles and reviewing 293 abstracts, 72 articles were included to identify the use cases

### Limitation:

> ([[Gonzales et al., 2023.pdf#page=9&annotation=976R|Gonzales et al., 2023, p.9]])
> However, there are still risks that some quantity of the original data could be leaked.
> ([[Gonzales et al., 2023.pdf#page=9&annotation=979R|Gonzales et al., 2023, p.9]])
> Not all [[Synthetische Patiendendaten|Synthetic data ]] replicate precisely the content and properties of the original dataset– making them less useful for generating conclusions about a population.
