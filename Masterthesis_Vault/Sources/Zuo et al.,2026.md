# [[Zuo et al.,2026.pdf|Pedagogical Efficacy of LLM-Generated Synthetic Data Versus Real-World Clinical Records: A Randomized Controlled Non-Inferiority Trial]]


### Research Gap:

> ([[Zuo et al.,2026.pdf#page=1&annotation=1381R|Zuo et al., 2026, p.1]])
> Expert-reviewed clinical cases generated by large language models (LLMs) may supplement case resources in medical education, but their short-term educational performance relative to real-case-derived teaching materials remains uncertain.

> ([[Zuo et al.,2026.pdf#page=2&annotation=1383R|Zuo et al., 2026, p.2]])
> Despite considerable enthusiasm, randomized studies directly comparing the educational effects of expert-reviewed AI-generated clinical cases with matched real cases remain scarce.

> ([[Zuo et al.,2026.pdf#page=2&annotation=1385R|Zuo et al., 2026, p.2]])
> Such teaching cases still derive from real patient information and should not be treated as synthetic data fully independent of real data

> ([[Zuo et al.,2026.pdf#page=2&annotation=1387R|Zuo et al., 2026, p.2]])
> Generated content may contain factual errors, illogical reasoning, or insufficient clinical cues, and may be especially limited in reproducing the ambiguity and uncertainty of complex cases.


### Methodik:

> ([[Zuo et al.,2026.pdf#page=1&annotation=1389R|Zuo et al., 2026, p.1]])
> We conducted a prospective, parallel-group, randomized non-inferiority trial.

Model: [[Gemini 3.0 Pro]]
> ([[Zuo et al.,2026.pdf#page=3&annotation=1391R|Zuo et al., 2026, p.3]])
> AI-generated clinical cases were produced with Gemini 3.0 Pro using matched, fully de-identified real cases as input

> ([[Zuo et al.,2026.pdf#page=3&annotation=1393R|Zuo et al., 2026, p.3]])
> Model outputs were not manually edited; cases that did not meet the review criteria were regenerated.

> ([[Zuo et al.,2026.pdf#page=3&annotation=1395R|Zuo et al., 2026, p.3]])
> Materials entered the formal study only after all three experts unanimously agreed that the case pair was matched and adequate.

> ([[Zuo et al.,2026.pdf#page=3&annotation=1397R|Zuo et al., 2026, p.3]])
> the five case pairs were classified into three levels of educational complexity

> ([[Zuo et al.,2026.pdf#page=1&annotation=1399R|Zuo et al., 2026, p.1]])
> A total of 403 participants were randomized


### Main Finding:

> ([[Zuo et al.,2026.pdf#page=1&annotation=1401R|Zuo et al., 2026, p.1]])
> Because the lower bound of the confidence interval was below the prespecified non-inferiority margin of –0.5 points, non-inferiority was not demonstrated

> ([[Zuo et al.,2026.pdf#page=8&annotation=1403R|Zuo et al., 2026, p.8]])
> Our findings therefore neither support the optimistic conclusion that AI-generated and real cases are interchangeable nor provide evidence that AI-generated cases are inferior.

> ([[Zuo et al.,2026.pdf#page=8&annotation=1405R|Zuo et al., 2026, p.8]])
> For Level 3 cases, AI-generated cases received lower realism ratings than real cases

> ([[Zuo et al.,2026.pdf#page=8&annotation=1407R|Zuo et al., 2026, p.8]])
> Among the 970 judgments in the AI-generated case group, the corresponding numbers were 498 (51.3%), 227 (23.4%), and 245 (25.3%), respectively

-> ca. die Hälfte der KI-Fälle wurde für echt gehalten

> ([[Zuo et al.,2026.pdf#page=8&annotation=1409R|Zuo et al., 2026, p.8]])
> LLM-generated cases tend to be more logically coherent and structured, which may help novices build frameworks but may also reduce exposure to authentic clinical uncertainty


### Limitation:

> ([[Zuo et al.,2026.pdf#page=10&annotation=1411R|Zuo et al., 2026, p.10]])
> it included only general surgery cases, so the conclusions may not generalize to other diseases, specialties, learning settings, or AI content that has not undergone expert review.

> ([[Zuo et al.,2026.pdf#page=10&annotation=1413R|Zuo et al., 2026, p.10]])
> the primary outcome was a 10-item immediate post-training test administered without a pre-training test, delayed follow-up, or performance-based assessment.

> ([[Zuo et al.,2026.pdf#page=10&annotation=1415R|Zuo et al., 2026, p.10]])
> the generation process is therefore not deterministically reproducible

> ([[Zuo et al.,2026.pdf#page=9&annotation=1417R|Zuo et al., 2026, p.9]])
> The AI-generated cases in this study used matched, fully de-identified real cases as input and therefore were not synthetic cases independent of real patient data.



### Zentrales Zitat:
> ([[Zuo et al.,2026.pdf#page=9&annotation=1419R|Zuo et al., 2026, p.9]])
> Expert supervision was therefore not an optional step in our study
