# [[Ling et al., 2026.pdf|MALLM-GAN: Multi-Agent Large Language Model as Generative Adversarial Network for Synthesizing Tabular Data]]

### Research Gap:

> ([[Ling et al., 2026.pdf#page=1&annotation=828R|Ling et al., 2026, p.1]])
> Generating synthetic (tabular) data can address this, but existing models typically require substantial amounts of data to train effectively, contradicting our objective of solving data scarcity.

> ([[Ling et al., 2026.pdf#page=1&annotation=830R|Ling et al., 2026, p.1]])
> However fine-tuning LLMs requires a larger sample size, contradicting the objective of addressing data scarcity.


### Methodik:

> ([[Ling et al., 2026.pdf#page=1&annotation=832R|Ling et al., 2026, p.1]])
> we propose a novel framework to generate synthetic tabular data, powered by large language models (LLMs) that emulates the architecture of a Generative Adversarial Network ([[GAN]]).

> ([[Ling et al., 2026.pdf#page=3&annotation=834R|Ling et al., 2026, p.3]])
> We propose to develop a multi-agent LLM as GAN (MALLM-GAN) that generates tabular data by mimicking adversarial optimization

3 Komponenten ([[Multi Agent Architektur|MAS]]):
- Generator (LLM): erzeugt Daten aus dem Data Generation Process θ + Few-Shot-Beispielen [[Ling et al., 2026.pdf#page=3&annotation=836R|Ling et al., 2026, p.3]]
  θ enthält u.a. eine kausale Struktur als DAG [[Ling et al., 2026.pdf#page=3&annotation=838R|Ling et al., 2026, p.3]]
- Discriminator (kein LLM, Logistic Regression) [[Ling et al., 2026.pdf#page=4&annotation=840R|Ling et al., 2026, p.4]]
- Optimizer (LLM): verbessert θ per Prompt-Optimierung [[Ling et al., 2026.pdf#page=2&annotation=842R|Ling et al., 2026, p.2]]

Models: [[GPT-4o]] (Generator + Optimizer) [[Ling et al., 2026.pdf#page=4&annotation=844R|Ling et al., 2026, p.4]], zusätzlich [[Qwen3]] und [[Llama 3]] [[Ling et al., 2026.pdf#page=5&annotation=846R|Ling et al., 2026, p.5]]

Datasets:
> ([[Ling et al., 2026.pdf#page=5&annotation=848R|Ling et al., 2026, p.5]])
> three public datasets (Adult(Becker and Kohavi, 1996), Medical Insurance(med, 2018), Asia(Scutari, 2009)), and two private medical datasets (ATACH2, ERICH)

> ([[Ling et al., 2026.pdf#page=5&annotation=850R|Ling et al., 2026, p.5]])
> Privacy leakage by Distance to Closest Records (DCR) and Machine Learning Efficiency (MLE)


### Main Finding:

> ([[Ling et al., 2026.pdf#page=5&annotation=852R|Ling et al., 2026, p.5]])
> MALLM-GAN generated highquality synthetic tabular data across multiple datasets and training data sizes, outperforming baselines

> ([[Ling et al., 2026.pdf#page=6&annotation=854R|Ling et al., 2026, p.6]])
> MALLM-GAN outperformed the baselines in both public and private datasets, suggesting that it does not rely on the pre-trained LLM’s memorization.

> ([[Ling et al., 2026.pdf#page=6&annotation=858R|Ling et al., 2026, p.6]])
> However, the LLM optimizer improved θ using prior knowledge encoded in LLM and finally achieved the highest MLE.

> ([[Ling et al., 2026.pdf#page=6&annotation=856R|Ling et al., 2026, p.6]])
> The MLE did not increase with more examples because more examples will increase the context length and the generator overlooks some key context information.

> ([[Ling et al., 2026.pdf#page=7&annotation=860R|Ling et al., 2026, p.7]])
> open-source models perform comparably to GPT-4o on public datasets but show a clear performance gap on more complex private datasets.

> ([[Ling et al., 2026.pdf#page=8&annotation=862R|Ling et al., 2026, p.8]])
> Baseline models failed to generate realistic samples due to limited data, whereas MALLM-GAN produced distributions closely matching the real data


### Limitation:

> ([[Ling et al., 2026.pdf#page=9&annotation=864R|Ling et al., 2026, p.9]])
> due to the limited context length of current large language models (LLMs), the method does not scale well to extremely high-dimensional datasets.

> ([[Ling et al., 2026.pdf#page=9&annotation=866R|Ling et al., 2026, p.9]])
> LLMs are known to struggle with high-quality random number generation

> ([[Ling et al., 2026.pdf#page=9&annotation=868R|Ling et al., 2026, p.9]])
> our method lacks theoretical guarantees on convergence

> ([[Ling et al., 2026.pdf#page=9&annotation=870R|Ling et al., 2026, p.9]])
> its relative performance gains diminish as the dataset size increases

> ([[Ling et al., 2026.pdf#page=9&annotation=872R|Ling et al., 2026, p.9]])
> synthetic samples generated by the model may not be statistically representative.



### Zentrales Zitat:
> ([[Ling et al., 2026.pdf#page=2&annotation=874R|Ling et al., 2026, p.2]])
> Explainability: Our LLM-based generator explicitly reveals the data generation process through prompt design.
